EII v1.5 2026

16 мин чтения

EII

Заголовок раздела «EII»

How to Read This Description

Заголовок раздела «How to Read This Description»

This text explains how EII (EII) processes information: which subjects they notice, how they interpret them, where they act confidently, where adaptation requires effort, what support they expect, and what they naturally offer to others.

Information metabolism describes our predispositions in perceiving and processing information about different aspects of the world. This description should not be read as a fixed set of traits or mandatory behavioral patterns. It is a generalized account of how EII’s psyche normally operates, while actual manifestations may vary. The same function can appear differently depending on a person’s experience, upbringing, environment, current condition, and individual characteristics. A match or mismatch with any single characteristic does not determine a person’s type.

The eight functions work together. They form four blocks of two functions, with one function taking priority and the other supporting it.

Each block follows the same structure:

  1. Block name — its place in the structure of the psyche.
  2. Role of the block — the tasks it performs.
  3. How the block manifests in EII — how its two functions work together in EII.
  4. Function name — its role within the block, followed by its more conventional name in parentheses.
  5. Information Element — the subject processed by the function.
  6. Role of the function — how it operates in the psyche.
  7. How it manifests in EII — how the function may appear in EII.

Brief Profile

Заголовок раздела «Brief Profile»

  • Core orientation: EII perceives the world as a system of relationships and notices people’s priorities and values. They seek harmonious relationships and morality. Abstract thinking helps them notice qualities and possibilities that have not yet emerged—what a person may become and how they may develop. This allows EII to understand hidden motives and find opportunities for personal growth and reconciliation.

  • Area of tension and duty: EII has difficulty with pressure, coercion, and the need to defend their position forcefully. Logical analysis and structuring require additional effort.

  • What supports and energizes them: practical help, clear methods, comfort, and care. A good quality of life helps EII work more effectively, contribute, and serve others.

  • What they offer others: help in regulating the emotional atmosphere, seeing future prospects, organizing time sensibly, and directing effort toward potentially useful activities.

EGO Block

Заголовок раздела «EGO Block»

Role of the Block

Заголовок раздела «Role of the Block»

The EGO block describes what is central to a person’s identity and the program of their thinking. Its outcome is expressed through the ideas of the program-potential function. The communicative function serves as an instrument for achieving its aims.

How the Block Manifests in EII

Заголовок раздела «How the Block Manifests in EII»

EII is a natural humanist and educator. They use abstract thinking to understand how to live morally, what is right, and what truly matters. Their central concern is helping people remain humane.

EII seeks to understand relationships, values, and people’s motives. Intuition helps them notice qualities and possibilities that have not yet emerged—what a person may become and how they may develop. This enables EII to find opportunities for growth, reconciliation, and improved relationships.

Program-Potential Function (Base)

Заголовок раздела «Program-Potential Function (Base)»

Information Element: ethics of relations (Fi)
Core subjects: relationships, priorities, subjective distance—the sense of closeness or distance between people

Role of the Function

Заголовок раздела «Role of the Function»

Forms the type’s primary picture of the world and sets the main direction of its attention. It determines the subjects and concepts through which a person primarily interprets events and the questions about which their own ideas most often arise.

How It Manifests in EII

Заголовок раздела «How It Manifests in EII»

EII perceives events through relationships between people, their attitudes toward situations, motives, and priorities. They notice what matters to whom, how close people are, and how the distance between them changes.

EII often reflects on morality: what is good, right, and acceptable in relationships. They seek to uphold moral standards and may help others, especially those close to them, form their priorities. They are inclined to educate others and to speak openly about their feelings and attitudes.

EII notices changes in closeness between people and tries to understand the causes of emotional cooling or tension. When a relationship deteriorates, they may increase the distance without becoming openly hostile. During conflict, they often look for a way to reconcile the parties or at least preserve mutual respect.

Communicative Function (Creative)

Заголовок раздела «Communicative Function (Creative)»

Information Element: intuition of possibilities (Ne)
Core subjects: patterns, potential, ideas, possibilities

Role of the Function

Заголовок раздела «Role of the Function»

Helps a person orient themselves in the world. They readily discuss the function’s subject and observe how others respond. This feedback helps refine their understanding of a situation. Through this subject, they also try to interest or motivate others.

How It Manifests in EII

Заголовок раздела «How It Manifests in EII»

EII works easily with deep, abstract ideas in the humanities, considers different courses of development, and estimates their likelihood. They are often interested in people’s qualities that have not yet emerged and in their potential for development.

EII uses intuition to find paths toward personal growth, reconciliation, and better relationships. When motivating someone, they draw attention to future prospects and available possibilities.

When discussing a person or a difficult situation, EII may suggest several possible explanations. They can notice a hidden ability and suggest where it might be useful. In relationships, they look for a possibility of repair before concluding that the situation is hopeless.

SUPEREGO Block

Заголовок раздела «SUPEREGO Block»

Role of the Block

Заголовок раздела «Role of the Block»

The SUPEREGO block is necessary for socialization. It includes weak functions that a person seeks to develop in order to meet the demands and norms of their environment.

How the Block Manifests in EII

Заголовок раздела «How the Block Manifests in EII»

EII may find it difficult to act under pressure, in conflict, or when they must defend their boundaries. They try to understand the rules and justify their position logically. These situations require additional effort and are tied to their ideas about duty, correctness, and acceptable limits of pressure.

When faced with pressure or conflict, EII tries to understand the rules of the situation, determine their rights, and provide a logical justification for their position. This helps them adapt to forceful interaction and preserve acceptable limits of pressure.

Adaptive-Load Function (Role)

Заголовок раздела «Adaptive-Load Function (Role)»

Information Element: structural logic (Ti)
Core subjects: structure, causes, space

Role of the Function

Заголовок раздела «Role of the Function»

Shows where a person feels pressure from the environment and a need to adapt. Through this function, they try to demonstrate the stability and soundness of their position.

How It Manifests in EII

Заголовок раздела «How It Manifests in EII»

EII may feel increased tension when their rights are not recognized or when they are dissatisfied with their place in a social hierarchy. In such situations, they try to shift from pressure toward a discussion of rules, rights, and responsibilities: to explain their position logically, justify their demands, and negotiate acceptable conditions. Logical analysis and structuring information may require effort, but they help EII adapt to the situation and preserve stability.

Conservative-Critical Function (Vulnerable)

Заголовок раздела «Conservative-Critical Function (Vulnerable)»

Information Element: force sensing (Se)
Core subjects: pressure, adaptation, forceful interaction

Role of the Function

Заголовок раздела «Role of the Function»

Preserves psychological integrity: a stable sense of what is permissible, right, and consistent with internalized principles. It provides a sense of being true to oneself. Violations and failures in this area may provoke intensely unpleasant feelings, a critical reaction, and a sense of duty. The function’s attitudes change slowly; developing it is difficult but important.

How It Manifests in EII

Заголовок раздела «How It Manifests in EII»

EII considers it their duty to hold an acceptable status in society. It is important to them that others respect their personal space and do not exert unnecessary pressure. They regard force as acceptable only in exceptional situations. They react painfully to negative judgments of their willpower, including their own judgments.

EII may find it difficult to refuse abruptly, issue a stern reprimand, or demand respect for their boundaries. They dislike coercing others and may spend a long time checking whether they truly have the right to act decisively. Under prolonged pressure, they may withdraw, increase the distance, or end the interaction.

SUPERID Block

Заголовок раздела «SUPERID Block»

Role of the Block

Заголовок раздела «Role of the Block»

The SUPERID block is called “childlike” because its subjects are areas in which a person especially needs environmental support and expects their needs to be met. Through this block, the type assesses how suitable and valuable the surrounding environment is. It is the block of rational self-interest.

How the Block Manifests in EII

Заголовок раздела «How the Block Manifests in EII»

EII expects assistance in performing or organizing work intended to improve their standard of living and needs support with practical household matters.

EII needs clear methods of action and practical help. Comfort, care, and physical well-being provide the energy required for effective work and service to others.

Expectation-Resource Function (Suggestive)

Заголовок раздела «Expectation-Resource Function (Suggestive)»

Information Element: business logic (Te)
Core subjects: technology, activity, efficiency

Role of the Function

Заголовок раздела «Role of the Function»

Reflects a person’s needs and shows which knowledge, advice, or assistance they particularly value and expect from others. Such support is perceived as a valuable resource.

How It Manifests in EII

Заголовок раздела «How It Manifests in EII»

EII seeks to understand the practical value of actions and often asks, “What is this for?” They may find it difficult to independently devise an enterprising solution or a more efficient way to handle a new task.

They value clear, specific, and visual instructions that explain the sequence of actions in accessible language. It is important for them to understand where to begin, what order to follow, and how to tell whether the work has been done well.

EII is interested in ways to make work more convenient, higher in quality, and more efficient. They value diligence in others and practical assistance with specific work.

Motivational-Mobilizing Function (Activating)

Заголовок раздела «Motivational-Mobilizing Function (Activating)»

Information Element: sensing of sensations (Si)
Core subjects: harmony, comfort, compatibility, quality

Role of the Function

Заголовок раздела «Role of the Function»

A person’s emotional state changes noticeably depending on how well things are going in this function’s sphere. Suitable conditions inspire and energize them, while unsatisfactory conditions demotivate them.

How It Manifests in EII

Заголовок раздела «How It Manifests in EII»

Comfort, physical well-being, and care noticeably affect EII’s emotional state. Suitable conditions inspire and energize them, while physical deprivation, fatigue, or poor health may undermine their motivation.

EII is attracted to beauty, harmony, convenience, appropriateness, and the quality of things. They enjoy receiving care and support. As they develop this function, they also learn to care more effectively for the comfort and well-being of others.

In everyday life, a comfortable workplace, good food, proper rest, and attention to physical needs help them preserve energy for work and helping people.

ID Block

Заголовок раздела «ID Block»

Role of the Block

Заголовок раздела «Role of the Block»

The ID block is the psyche’s realization block. Through it, a person gives society their time and labor, helps through action, and creates a useful product.

How the Block Manifests in EII

Заголовок раздела «How the Block Manifests in EII»

EII may focus on the emotional background of a situation and regulate it in order to engage another person and inspire them with a sense of future possibility.

EII regulates the emotional tone to support an activity in which they see meaning and future prospects. They may inspire someone, help them avoid wasting time, and direct their attention toward what seems timely and meaningful.

Imperative-Temporal Function (Ignoring)

Заголовок раздела «Imperative-Temporal Function (Ignoring)»

Information Element: ethics of emotions (Fe)
Core subjects: emotions, state

Role of the Function

Заголовок раздела «Role of the Function»

Automatically brings a person into a working state and helps assess whether events are promising. Information that does not serve the realization-technological function may seem unpromising and feel like a distraction.

How It Manifests in EII

Заголовок раздела «How It Manifests in EII»

EII is sensitive to the emotional tone of communication and may unconsciously adapt to the other person’s state. They regulate the atmosphere to support meaningful and promising activity.

When they notice tension or irritation, EII may speak more calmly, let the person express themselves, or gently alter the emotional tone of the conversation. They try not to create unnecessary emotional noise and restrain similar expressions in others when they believe those feelings are distracting from useful action.

They do not seek to maintain emotional activity for its own sake. Mood and expressiveness become meaningful when they help someone feel connected with loved ones, see future prospects, become inspired, or move on to action.

Realization-Technological Function (Demonstrative)

Заголовок раздела «Realization-Technological Function (Demonstrative)»

Information Element: intuition of time (Ni)
Core subjects: time, meaning, future prospects, routine

Role of the Function

Заголовок раздела «Role of the Function»

Through this function, a person provides their labor to others. When there is a need, they can perform the work independently or suggest a specific way of achieving a result.

How It Manifests in EII

Заголовок раздела «How It Manifests in EII»

EII helps others orient themselves in time: see future prospects, choose an appropriate moment to act, and avoid wasting time. They may advise what should be done and when, helping establish a suitable routine.

They take time and agreements seriously. They try to arrive on time, avoid keeping others waiting, and fulfill promises by the agreed deadline. They may remind someone about a meeting, obligation, or necessary preparation and help calculate the available time without rushing or being late.

In a difficult situation, EII can support someone with a positive forecast, show how present actions may lead to a favorable future, and help them see the meaning of events and situations. If an activity appears to have no future, they may warn against wasting time on it.

Traits and Groups of EII

Заголовок раздела «Traits and Groups of EII»

Introvert

Заголовок раздела «Introvert»

EII’s attention is directed toward the internal and implicit characteristics of objects, people, and their relationships. They prefer depth of coverage to breadth—quality matters more than quantity.

Farsighted

Заголовок раздела «Farsighted»

EII relies on accumulated experience and tries to think situations through in advance. Experience itself is valuable as a resource that can later be relied upon. They tend to make plans for the future so that they can depend on what was anticipated beforehand.

Intuitive

Заголовок раздела «Intuitive»

EII thinks abstractly and in general terms, seeking to grasp the essence of a phenomenon. Specific details attract them less than an overall understanding. Even when discussing something concrete, they may use broad formulations.

Aristocratic

Заголовок раздела «Aristocratic»

EII tends to assess objects through their membership in a group and automatically attributes the group’s characteristics to them. Such characteristics may be called inclusive: they are “included” in an object through its group membership. In other words, a characteristic of the group is transferred to every object belonging to it. If a group is considered to possess a certain quality, that quality is automatically attributed to its individual members.

Positivist

Заголовок раздела «Positivist»

EII primarily notices positive qualities and speaks about what is present. This may appear as insufficient attention to negative aspects of a situation or object.

Yielding

Заголовок раздела «Yielding»

When setting a goal, EII keeps in mind the resources they are prepared to spend on it. If the goal proves unattainable, they are predisposed to redirect those resources without unnecessary loss. They readily compromise, especially to preserve relationships, while tending to expect a concession in return.

Ethical

Заголовок раздела «Ethical»

EII confidently navigates emotions and relationships. They feel more assured in the humanities than in analytical matters. In communication, they express emotional and moral evaluations more readily than logical conclusions.

Serious

Заголовок раздела «Serious»

EII is more interested in discussing practical methods and concrete work than theoretical structures. They tend to consult authoritative sources and consider their practical application. When explaining something, they prefer examples and methodological guidance. In relationships, deep structure and correctness matter more to them than emotional ease, so they grow close to new people gradually.

Constructivist

Заголовок раздела «Constructivist»

In communication, EII first perceives the semantic content of a message and only then its emotional tone. The same applies to their own speech: they first consider what to say, while intonation and delivery adjust afterward. Constructivists’ emotional states are inert and change slowly.

Process Type

Заголовок раздела «Process Type»

For EII, the path toward a goal is more important than the result itself. They are oriented toward evolutionary development and maintaining a process.

Declaring

Заголовок раздела «Declaring»

EII tends to speak in monologues without expecting a response. They seek simplicity and concision and can be difficult to interrupt. They often divide statements into short, definite parts: “Once said, it is settled.”

Judicious

Заголовок раздела «Judicious»

EII prefers to weigh all options before acting. The stage of reflection is no less important than implementation because it is where the most harmonious possibility is selected. They prefer a comfortable mode over active mobilization and readily enter a relaxed state.

Strategist

Заголовок раздела «Strategist»

EII finds it easier to define a goal than to devise concrete steps toward it. Strategists’ goals and needs are more stable and less likely to change with context; what changes are the means of achieving them.

Rational

Заголовок раздела «Rational»

EII relies more on their own judgments than on information arriving from outside. In practice, this appears as a need for planning and inertia in already established plans. Changes of plan may cause discomfort.

Static

Заголовок раздела «Static»

EII perceives events as a sequence of separate, completed pictures rather than a continuous flow. This kind of perception is also called discrete. In speech, it appears as a transition from one completed picture to another. They tend to use verbs of state: “is,” “is located,” and “relates to.”

Delta Quadra

Заголовок раздела «Delta Quadra»

Delta Quadra values maturity and the golden mean: building things to last and arriving at rules that everyone will eventually recognize. It values judiciousness, intelligence, stable and respected work, and kind, well-developed relationships.

Soulful Communication Style

Заголовок раздела «Soulful Communication Style»

EII considers an interaction successful when information about relationships and priorities has been exchanged, new values have formed, and relationships have been adjusted. They are predisposed to educate others.
